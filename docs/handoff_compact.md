## 2. The study (one paragraph)
Send the change an edit makes in GPT-2 small (layer 8, SAE `gpt2-small-res-jb`) into GPT-2 medium (and the reverse) at inference time, through a translator fitted on ordinary text, no weights changed. **Export** = edit in small, write into medium. **Import** = read medium's state, write into small (the user's original idea: a stronger model teaching a weaker one). Translator = map of the residual stream `blocks.L.hook_resid_pre` (not SAE features); SAE only needed where the edit is made.

## 3. What was done and the key numbers
- **A0** setup: tokenizers identical, fidelity 1e-4, all OK. **A1** 50 linear translators (small L 2,4,6,8,10 x medium L 4,8,12,16,20, both directions), per-dim R2 small8->medium16 0.58, medium16->small8 0.66; gradient descent = exact (0.6287 vs 0.6289). **A2** stitching recovers 91 to 98%; difference cosine at the changed word 0.63 to 0.76, at the last position 0.24 to 0.31 (above chance); Gate 1 (0.3 line) passes only medium->small.
- **B1 country swap** (Gate 2 fixed beforehand, PASS both directions): small->medium 55%, medium->small 33% swapped capital becomes top answer; controls 0 to 1%; other layer pairs 36 to 79% (earlier layers better). It is a control of the channel, mostly the changed word's position; not an edit, not a fact.
- **D controlled export** (50 dev + 150 test CounterFact counterfactual records; 88 test records whose edit reached rank 1 in small are the primary set; edits: 116/200 reach rank 1 in small): **Gate 3 FAILS** (written before the run: (a) top-1 at the dev dose >= 15%; (b) >= 10 points above the strongest control; (c) paired Wilcoxon p < 0.01). Linear: top-1 5% (medium L12, dose 1.5) and 8% (L16, dose 2.0); median rank of the target 142 -> 23 and 14; rewordings PS (new beats true) 36% -> 51/52% (+15, +16 points; about 60% of the source edit's own lift in small); neighbours NS (true still beats new) 67% -> 62/63% (-4 to -5); random vector and another record's edit 0%; rank-matched random-word edit 0 to 3% (strongest control, 5% at its best dose); word push 83% top-1 at L16 but NS 23%; fact stated in medium's prompt PS 97% but NS 44%; unrelated prompts: another record's edit disturbs as much as the translated edit; norm-matched dose (outcome-free) agrees. Plausible counterfactual target gains more than a rank-matched random word (2.04 vs 1.49, p=0.002).
- **Neural translator** (residual MLP started at the linear solution): per-dim R2 0.59->0.70, 0.58->0.68, 0.66->0.75; changed-word cosine +0.09 to +0.12; last-position cosine only +0.006 to +0.018 (chance level rises as much). Export with it: top-1 6% and 8%, rank 19 and 14, PS +18 and +14: **no better** than linear (H29 rejected).
- **Phase C** (small alone, 150 test records): the pre-stated rule applied as written gives **read-out steering** (rewordings gain 1.04 vs 0.82 for a rank-matched random-word edit, p = 0.032, not < 0.01; subject-only edit succeeds on 12% vs 59% for all positions; last-only 7%), BUT the edit is not dominated by the target's output direction (logit-lens rank 4,922, cosine 0.05), where subject-only edits succeed they carry to rewordings (81%), and the masked edits may be limited by the optimiser. Weak evidence either way. **Withdrawn:** my Entry 31 claim that the edit is "mainly a change of the subject's representation" (89% of the change is at positions between the start token and last, which include relation words).
- Hypotheses: H24 (fact edit) not supported as written, weak; H25 partially supported; H26 supported (channel control); H27 (export) not supported at the pre-set bar; H28 (Import) untested; H29 (neural better) rejected. Experiments EXP-023 to EXP-029 in `research/experiment_index.md`.

## 4. What I last told the user (so I stay consistent)
- **Why not just a neural net / what the problem is:** the map exists for ordinary states (R2 0.7, stitching 91 to 99%); the issue is carrying a *small, unusual edit* through it. Candidate causes (hypotheses, NOT tested): (1) wrong training target: squared error on whole states is dominated by common structure (word identity, position, syntax), the edit lives in low-variance directions (consistent with word-level cosine improving but last-position not); (2) the edit is "a key cut for one lock": tuned by gradient through small's later layers, not necessarily the fact's meaning, and medium's later layers read state differently; (3) the translator never saw edited states (edit is about 29% of residual norm at the last position); (4) one injection layer, one map. The network result rules out "just needs nonlinearity on ordinary text".
- **Pass mark (Gate 3):** three pre-set rules; 15% top-1 was arbitrary (set after the 30-record pilot, a minimal "doing something real above controls" bar, not a "works" bar; the user rightly said it is low). It failed even that, so a stricter bar changes nothing for this result. The real yardstick is the source: the edit is top-1 in small on 100% of those 88 records. **I proposed (not yet written into the plan) a bar for any rerun: top-1 >= 50% and >= 80% of the edit's own effect on rewordings in small**, written before the run. I asked "shall I put it in the plan?"; no answer yet.
