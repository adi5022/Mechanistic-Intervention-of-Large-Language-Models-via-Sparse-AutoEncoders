Two notes written after the fact do not change any verdict: the 5-point neighbour line of Gate 5 was stricter than the source edit's own loss of 9 points inside small, so Gates 6, 7 and 9 use the edit's own loss; and the dose rule (dev top-1 on 28 records) was replaced by the dev log-rank gain from step D10 on, after step D9b showed it to be fragile.
