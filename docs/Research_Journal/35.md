# Research Journal Entry 35: Import, steps E2 to E4: small with medium's translated state

**Date:** October 10, 2026 (night)
**Branch:** `cross-model-transfer`
**Status:** The Import experiment is done and **fails the pre-set Gate 8**. Replacing small's layer-8 state with medium's layer-16 state, translated by the A1 map, carries real information (it rescues 34% of the 780 facts that small gets wrong, against about 0% for the controls) but it costs more than it gains: it breaks facts small already knew, and small's accuracy over all facts falls from 8.3% to 7.2%.
**Builds on:** Entry 34 (the count of 780 candidates). **Plan:** `docs/cross_model_transfer/PLAN.md` (steps E2 to E4, Gate 8). **Data pack:** `docs/Research_Journal/packs/cross_model_transfer/e2_import.json`; log `logs/e2_import.log`. **Figure:** `docs/Research_Journal/images/e35_fig1_import.png|svg`.

---

## 1. Why
Import was the original aim of the study: a stronger model teaches a weaker one at inference time, with no weight change. Entry 34 found 780 true facts that medium gets right and small gets wrong. This entry tests whether medium's state, written into small, makes small answer them, without telling small the answer.

## 2. Method (`tools/transfer/19_import_experiment.py`, `src/transfer/import_eval.py`; new code only; nothing is trained)
The design, the arms, the controls, Gate 8 and the readings were written in the plan before the tool was built; the bar was raised from 20% to 50% and the primary arm changed to replacement after the author's comments (revision note in the plan), the numbers were confirmed by the author, and the plan and tool were committed (`1719459`) before the run.
- **Primary arm R, replacement (the "stitched model"):** at every position but the first, small's layer-8 state is replaced by medium's layer-16 state translated into small's coordinates, T(h) = (h - mu_src) W + mu_dst with the A1 map `m2s_L16_L8`; small's layers 8 to 11 and output then run as normal. Run on all 21,789 single-token CounterFact facts outside the ROME dev and pilot records. Small is never told the answer.
- **Controls (on the 780 candidates):** C1 a state from another sentence of the same token length; C2 the translated average medium state (the information destroyed); C3 a random matrix of the same scale instead of the map.
- **Secondary arms (candidates split into 100 dev and 680 test with a fixed seed; k and alpha chosen on dev by gain):** F, the full difference added (alpha in {0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 3}); S, the SAE-filtered difference (small's layer-8 SAE, k in {5, 20, 100} features per position); a word push of S's size toward the true answer (reported; it uses the answer).
- **Exploratory:** replacement for four other layer pairs, not used to choose anything.
- **Gate 8 (confirmed):** (a) rescue at least 50% of the candidates; (b) accuracy over all facts at least halfway between small's and medium's (9.09%); (c) at least 10 points above the strongest control with a paired test p < 0.01; (d) at least 90% of the "both right" facts stay right. Reading fixed in advance: all four = knowledge carried; (a) fails but agreement with medium's answers rises by 10 points = steering without knowledge; and so on (see the plan).

## 3. Results
Baselines (no change): small right on 1,809 facts (8.30%), medium on 2,152 (9.88%); groups: candidates 780, both right 1,372, small right and medium wrong 437, both wrong 19,200 (the counts of Entry 34 are reproduced). Small's top answer equals medium's on 71.1% of the facts.

| What replacement does | Facts | Result |
|---|---|---|
| candidates (small wrong, medium right) | 780 | **262 become right (33.6%)** |
| both right | 1,372 | 1,074 stay right (78.3%), 298 are lost |
| small right, medium wrong | 437 | 133 stay right (30.4%), 304 are lost |
| both wrong | 19,200 | 92 become right |
| all facts: accuracy | 21,789 | small 8.30%, medium 9.88%, **stitched 7.17%** (1,561 right, net -248) |

Controls (rescue of the 780): wrong-sentence state 0.8%, average state 0.0%, random translator 0.0%; paired test of R against the strongest control: R right and control wrong on 259 candidates, the reverse on 3, p = 4e-73.

Gate 8: (a) rescue 33.6% against 50%: **NO**; (b) accuracy 7.2% against the halfway line 9.1%: **NO**; (c) 32 points above the strongest control with p = 4e-73: ok; (d) survival of the both-right facts 78.3% against 90%: **NO** -> **FAIL**. Reading fixed in advance (as implemented): (a) fails, small's agreement with medium's answers rises only from 71.1% to 74.7% (3.6 points, under the 10 that counts as clear steering) and no secondary arm does better: "Import with this map does not work".
Note added after the result, not a change to the gate or the reading: the replacement does carry real, sentence-specific information (the controls rescue about nothing and the paired test is overwhelming); what fails is the net effect.

Other results:
- **Steering toward medium:** agreement of small's top answer with medium's 71.1% (small alone) to 74.7% (stitched); of the facts small knew and medium got wrong (437), 70% are lost, so small partly inherits medium's mistakes.
- **Rewordings:** on 1,560 rewordings of the candidates the true answer is top-1 for small 13.8%, medium 29.6%, stitched 13.1%: no gain on rewordings (medium itself knows only 30% of them).
- **Unrelated prompts:** KL(small || stitched) 0.17, top-1 flips 33% on 12 neutral prompts.
- **Secondary arms (680 test candidates):** F at alpha 1.0 (chosen on dev; the same operation as R) rescues 33.8%; S (k = 100, alpha 0.25 chosen on dev) rescues only 6.3% (gain 0.09): the SAE-filtered difference loses almost all the signal; the word push of S's size rescues 66.6% (it uses the answer).
- **Other layer pairs (exploratory):** replacement rescue / survival of both-right / accuracy: m2s_L16_L8 33.6% / 78.3% / 7.2%; m2s_L12_L8 7.4% / 40.6% / 3.2%; m2s_L8_L6 3.8% / 35.6% / 2.7%; m2s_L4_L2 5.6% / 57.6% / 4.9%; m2s_L20_L10 36.0% / 69.8% / 6.4%. The pair chosen beforehand is the best of them on survival and accuracy; the deepest donor layer rescues about as many.
- **Who is rescued (R, candidates):** by small's starting rank of the true answer: rank 2: 137 of 320 (43%); ranks 3 to 5: 84 of 236 (36%); 6 to 20: 36 of 155 (23%); over 20: 5 of 69 (7%). By medium's confidence: at least 0.5 on 54 facts, 35 rescued (65%); below 0.5 on 726 facts, 227 rescued (31%). By answer (rescued of total): 'French' 18/64, 'Apple' 17/45, 'English' 16/38, 'Antarctica' 15/37, 'Islam' 9/31, 'Russian' 10/26, 'Microsoft' 10/23, 'France' 13/19, 'London' 8/18, 'Dutch' 4/17: no single word dominates.

## 4. What this shows and does not show
Shows (this map, GPT-2 small and medium, single-token CounterFact facts):
- Medium's translated state carries real information about the sentence into small: one in three of the facts small gets wrong become right, and the controls do not.
- Full replacement is too blunt: it breaks about a fifth of the facts both models knew and most of the facts only small knew, so small's accuracy over all facts falls.
- The effect is strongest when small was close (rank 2) and medium was sure (at least 0.5).
- An SAE-filtered difference (small's SAE, the original idea) does not carry the signal.
Does not show: that Import can improve small overall (no arm did); that the effect transfers to rewordings; anything about a partial replacement (a dose between none and full, positions, gating by medium's confidence), which was not tested (the dose of arm F was chosen on rescue, not on net accuracy); other models or relations; a gain without running medium on the same sentence (Import needs medium's state at test time).

## 5. Mistakes and corrections
- The first draft of the plan had a 20% bar and a filtered arm as primary; both were changed on the author's argument before the build (revision note in the plan).
- A one-line bug in the tool (adding a generator to a tuple) was caught in the rehearsal.

## 6. What could be done next (not decided)
Soften the replacement: a dial between no change and full replacement scored on NET accuracy (the dose of F was chosen on rescue only), and use medium's state only where medium is confident (the rescue rate doubles there); a partial replacement (last position or subject span). All cheap and built on the same code. Alternatively, write up Import as a partial result with its costs.
