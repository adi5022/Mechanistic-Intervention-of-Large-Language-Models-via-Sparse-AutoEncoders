## Executive Summary

To eliminate GPU underutilization caused by serial Python execution overhead, we redesigned the candidate evaluation pipeline of the Layer Intervention Benchmark. By stacking candidates into the batch dimension of single PyTorch model calls (`batch_tokens = tokens.repeat(N, 1)`), we reduced the total model forward calls per prompt×layer evaluation from **126 down to 6** ($21\times$ reduction in forward call dispatch overhead) while maintaining **100% bit-exact equivalence** on selected features and safety decisions.

| `forward_passes` (`total_model_forwards`) | Number of actual `model(...)` / `run_with_hooks` calls dispatched (wall-clock unit). | 126 | **6** |

## 2. Implemented Sub-Steps & Verification Gates

### Step 3A — Pre-Batching Cleanup
- **Changes**: Dead-code removal in `src/editing.py`, fixed double SAE encode in `make_ablation_hook` (`src/hooks.py`), and introduced `CleanContext` clean-pass caching in `src/editing.py` and `src/benchmark/layer_benchmark_runner.py`.
- **Validation**: Exact match at `atol=1e-9`. Total model forward passes reduced from **126 to 122**.

### Step 3B — Batched Causal Ranking Stages
- **Changes**: Created `src/batched_eval.py` (`batched_ablation_probs`, `batched_ablation_probs_and_ranks` with `MAX_EVAL_BATCH = 32` chunking), added `make_per_row_scale_hook` to `src/hooks.py`, and added `use_batched` kwarg to `get_top_competitor_features` and `get_top_target_features`.
- **Validation**: Verified ordered feature-ID lists match element-by-element with 0 misorderings across 6 ROME prompts. On-device rank formula `(probs > target_prob).sum(dim=-1) + 1` matched `argsort` oracle exactly. Reduced ranking stage forward passes from **60 to 2**.

### Step 3C — Batched Safety Filter Stages
- **Changes**: Implemented `check_target_safe_batch` (`scale = 1 - strength`) and `check_boost_safe_batch` (`scale = 1 + strength`) in `src/editing.py`. Integrated batched safety checking into Stage 4 of `src/benchmark/layer_benchmark_runner.py`.
- **Validation**: Hard gate passed with **60 / 60 (100%) safety boolean agreement** on canonical case (`Layer 8`, MIT $\rightarrow$ Cambridge).
