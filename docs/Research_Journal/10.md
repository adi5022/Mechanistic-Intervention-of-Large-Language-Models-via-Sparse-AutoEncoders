# Research Journal Entry 10: Layer Intervention Depth Characterization Benchmark

**Date:** August 4, 2026  
**Status:** Initial Benchmark Characterization Sweep Completed  

---

## 1. Layman's Summary & Purpose

Until now, FeatureScalpel operated under the unverified assumption that **Layer 8** is the optimal location for SAE feature steering and intervention.

To experimentally characterize how intervention effectiveness changes across transformer depth, we implemented the dedicated **Layer Intervention Benchmark** (`layer_benchmark.py` and `src/benchmark/layer_benchmark_runner.py`).

This entry documents the initial proof-of-concept characterization sweep across layers `[2, 5, 8, 10]` on the standard Cambridge benchmark prompt:
- **Prompt**: `"The location of Massachusetts Institute of Technology is in"` -> Target `" Cambridge"`

## 4. Key Observations & Research Progress

1. **Non-Monotonic Depth Effectiveness**:
   - Intervention performance peaks at **Layer 8**, yielding the highest target probability (+2.37%) and highest rank recovery (rising from Rank 8 to Rank 3).
   - Early layers (Layer 2) show minimal response (+0.07%), while late layers (Layer 10) show intermediate response (+0.82%).
2. **Infrastructure Validation**:
   - The decoupled benchmark engine (`src/benchmark/layer_benchmark_runner.py`) automatically persists JSON research artifacts to `benchmark_results/` and supports seamless evaluation across layer depths.
