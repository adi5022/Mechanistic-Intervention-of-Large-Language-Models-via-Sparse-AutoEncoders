**Date:** October 11, 2026

## Part 1. E5: a softer Import

### 1.1 Why
Entry 35: replacing small's layer-8 state with medium's translated layer-16 state rescues 33.6% of the 780 facts small gets wrong, but loses 22% of the facts both models knew and 70% of those only small knew, so small's accuracy over all facts falls (8.3% to 7.2%). The replacement is too blunt. E5 asks whether a dial between no change and full replacement, or using medium's state only where medium is confident, makes Import net-positive.

### 1.2 Method (`tools/transfer/23_softer_import.py`; no training; 304 s)
Same 21,789 single-token facts (outside the ROME dev and pilot records), pair `m2s_L16_L8`, small is never told the answer. **Arm P(alpha):** small's layer-8 state at every position but the first becomes (1 - alpha) h_S + alpha T(h_M), alpha in {0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 0.85, 1.0} (alpha = 1 is the Entry 35 replacement). **Arm G(alpha, tau):** P(alpha) only where unchanged medium's top-1 probability at the last position is at least tau in {0.1, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7}. The facts are split once (seed 11) into **half A (10,894 facts), which chooses the setting, and half B (10,895), which reports it**: the setting with the highest overall accuracy on A among those that keep at least 90% of the "both right" facts. **Reading fixed in advance:** gain over small's accuracy on B of at least 0.5 points, both-right survival at least 90% and p < 0.01 (paired exact test) = a softer Import is net-positive; a gain above 0 that misses a condition = marginal; no gain = no softer variant helps.

### 1.3 Results
Baselines on B: small 8.5%, medium 10.2%. On all 21,789 facts: small 8.3%, medium 9.9%.

| alpha (all facts) | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.3 | **0.5** | 0.7 | 0.85 | 1.0 (replacement) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| accuracy | 8.6% | 8.9% | 9.2% | **9.3%** | 9.0% | 8.3% | 7.2% |
| rescue of the 780 candidates | 6.4% | 14.5% | 21.7% | 29.6% | 32.7% | 34.1% | 33.6% |
| both-right facts kept | 99.9% | 99.6% | 99.1% | 97.9% | 94.0% | 88.6% | 78.3% |

Chosen on half A: **P(alpha = 0.5)** (accuracy 9.2% on A; 42 settings compared). Reported on half B: accuracy **9.5%** (small alone 8.5%, medium alone 10.2%), gain **+1.00 points**, rescue 28.6% of the candidates, both-right kept 98.3%, small-right-only kept 80.5%, agreement of small's top answer with medium's 76.2% (small alone 71.0%). Paired exact test against small alone on B: the mix is right and small wrong on 162 facts, the reverse on 53, one-sided p = 2.5e-14. **Reading (fixed in advance): A SOFTER IMPORT IS NET-POSITIVE.** Gating by medium's confidence did not beat the plain dial (the best gated setting on A, G(0.5, 0.1), reached 9.0% against 9.2%).

### 1.4 What this shows and does not show
Shows: with the A1 map and no training, mixing medium's translated state into small at half weight makes small better overall, by about one point of accuracy (60% of the way from small to medium on B: 1.0 of 1.7 points), at the cost of 20% of the facts only small knew. This is the first net-positive Import result, with no SAE involved.
Does not show: that Import meets the old Gate 8 (it does not: rescue 28.6% against the 50% bar, and E5 is a descriptive diagnostic with its own rule); that the gain is large (one point); anything for rewordings (not measured here); a gain without running medium on the same sentence (needed at test time); other model pairs, relations or multi-token answers. The setting was chosen on half A of the same facts as B (a fixed split, one seed), so the choice is honest but the gain is for this fact pool; the controls of Entry 35 (wrong-sentence donor, average state, random map) were not repeated for the mix.

## Part 2. S1: does small's SAE matter for steering medium?

### 2.1 Why
D6 steers medium, which has no SAE, to 95% top-1 with 200 dials on small's SAE decoder directions. The author asked whether the SAE is needed at all. It was never tested.

### 2.2 Method (`tools/transfer/24_sae_basis_control.py`, `src/transfer/basis_control.py`; 1,677 s)
D6 repeated with everything the same (150 test records, same 200 candidate features per record so the same activation pattern at every position, medium layer 16, dose 2.0, 100 steps, Adam 0.1, KL weight 3.0, size weight 0.005, margin 0.3, same judging) except that the 200 decoder directions are replaced by one fixed Gaussian random matrix (seed 0; each row rescaled to the norm of the corresponding decoder row), the same rows for every prompt. Arms: random basis tuned toward the real counterfactual target, toward the rank-matched random word, and another record's recipe. **Reading fixed in advance (88 primary records):** the SAE basis matters if D6's top-1 exceeds the random basis's by at least 10 points or its rewordings lift by at least 5 points; no evidence it matters if both are smaller; the random basis is better if it exceeds D6's top-1 by 10 points.

### 2.3 Results (88 primary records; 150 in brackets)
| | Top-1 | Median rank | Rewordings (new beats true) | Neighbours (true still beats new) | Unrelated KL / flips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| medium unchanged | 0% | 142 | 36% | 67% | 0 / 0% |
| D6 with the SAE directions | **95%** (84%) | 1 | 60% | 59% | 0.024 / 11% |
| **random directions, real target** | **81%** (63%) | 1 | 58% | 57% | 0.020 / 7% |
| random directions, another record's recipe | 0% (0%) | 124 | 34% | 67% | 0.024 / 8% |
| D6, random-word target (own word) | 83% (67%) | 1 | 42% | 64% | |
| random directions, random-word target | 62% (45%) | 1 | 39% | 64% | 0.021 / 9% |

Difference (SAE minus random basis): top-1 **+15 points** (14.8), rewordings lift +2 points (2.3). **Reading fixed in advance: the SAE basis MATTERS** (the top-1 difference is at least 10 points; the rewordings difference is under 5).

### 2.4 What this shows and does not show
Shows: the SAE directions make the per-sentence tuning clearly more effective on the tuned prompt (95% against 81%; with a random-word target 83% against 62%). They are not what makes steering medium possible: 200 random directions with the same firing pattern already reach 81% top-1, put another record's recipe at 0%, and give the same rewordings and neighbour behaviour (58% against 60%, 57% against 59%) as the SAE directions. Together with S2 and S3 (the rewordings effect is a push toward the targeted word) the SAE is a convenient set of knobs, not special access to medium.
Does not show: that no SAE-free steering works (the random basis keeps the SAE's activations, i.e. which feature fires where and how much; only the 200 directions are random); anything about other random draws (one seed); a transfer (D6-type tuning looks at medium for every sentence).

## 3. Mistakes and corrections

## 4. What could be done next (not decided)
E5 suggests Import is where there is something to build on: (1) repeat the mix with the controls of Entry 35 and on rewordings and other relations; (2) learn the Import map for the mix (a map trained so that small's answers become right, the Import analogue of D7); (3) a per-position or per-layer mix. S1 needs no follow-up for the paper except a second random draw if the claim is to be stated numerically.
